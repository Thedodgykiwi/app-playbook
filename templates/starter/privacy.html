{{APP_NAME}} keeps only what it needs to work. No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

Using it without signing in

Everything you add is saved only on this device, in your browser's storage. Nobody else can see it, and it stays there until you delete it or clear your browser's data.

When you sign in with Google

  • From Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo, to show who's signed in.
  • Your data ({{WHAT IS SAVED, e.g. "your to-dos and lists"}}) is saved to your account in Google's Firebase service (stored in {{REGION, e.g. Europe}}), so it's on all your devices.
  • Only you can read or change it: the app's security rules block everyone else.
  • Your settings (theme, colours, text size) stay on each device.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: Menu → Settings → Delete my account removes your account and everything saved in it, straight away, on every version of the app.
  • This device: delete items in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Cookies and tracking

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's storage only to work (your data, your settings, and a copy of the app so it opens offline).
